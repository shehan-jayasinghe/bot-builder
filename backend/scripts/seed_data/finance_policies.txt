Nova Trust Bank — Finance Management Policies

Savings accounts — urban vs rural minimum balance
- Urban savings accounts require a minimum average monthly balance of ₹10,000.
- If the urban minimum balance is not maintained, a non-maintenance fee of ₹350 + GST applies per month.
- Rural savings accounts require a minimum average monthly balance of ₹5,000; non-maintenance fee is ₹150 + GST.

Loan EMI payments
- EMI due date is the 5th of each month.
- A grace period of 5 calendar days applies before late fees.
- If an EMI is missed after the grace period, a late fee of 2% of the EMI amount applies.
- Customers facing hardship should contact Collections to discuss payment plans or restructuring.
- Payment plans: customers may request a 3, 6, or 12 month restructuring plan if they have missed no more than two consecutive EMIs.

Savings interest
- Savings account interest: 4.0% p.a. on balances above ₹1,000, credited quarterly.

Overdraft protection
- Linked savings account transfers in ₹1,000 increments when the linked current account balance falls below zero.

Refund policy
- Merchant disputes are reviewed within 7 business days. Approved refunds post within 3–5 business days.

Contact finance support for balance inquiries, payment reminders, and hardship programs.
