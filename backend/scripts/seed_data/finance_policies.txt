ABC Bank — Finance Management Policies

Loan EMI payments are due on the 5th of each month. A grace period of 3 calendar days applies before late fees.

Late payment fee: USD 25 per missed EMI after the grace period.

Payment plans: Customers may request a 3, 6, or 12 month restructuring plan if they have missed no more than two consecutive EMIs.

Savings account interest: 4.25% APY on balances above USD 1,000.

Overdraft protection: Linked savings account transfers in USD 100 increments when checking balance falls below zero.

Refund policy: Merchant disputes are reviewed within 7 business days. Approved refunds post within 3–5 business days.

Contact finance support for balance inquiries, payment reminders, and hardship programs.
